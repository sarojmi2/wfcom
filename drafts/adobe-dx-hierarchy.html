Adobe Experience Cloud / CX Enterprise
Rebranded at Adobe Summit 2026 · AI-first · Agent-based architecture
Adobe's unified digital experience platform, rebranded as Adobe CX Enterprise at Summit 2026. Merges Creative Cloud and Experience Cloud under a single AI-first, agent-based architecture powered by AEP. Used by 74%+ of Fortune 500 companies.
